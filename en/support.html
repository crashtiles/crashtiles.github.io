Crash Tiles – Support & Help

If you need help or have questions regarding Crash Tiles, please review the troubleshooting steps below.

App Not Opening or Crashing

Force close and reopen the app. Make sure your device has enough free storage space and is running the latest version of iOS. If the issue persists, please contact us with your iOS version and device model.

Ads & Audio Issues

Check your active internet connection. Advertisements are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and availability may vary based on region or network status. Audio settings can be adjusted anytime via the in-game options.
