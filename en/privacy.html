Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Data Collection

Crash Tiles does not directly collect, store, or share personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. Game progress, high scores, and settings are saved locally on your device (AppStorage / UserDefaults).

Advertising Partners (Google Mobile Ads)

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. AdMob may process non-personally identifiable technical identifiers (such as IDFA or device telemetry) to serve personalized or contextual advertisements and measure performance.

Children's Privacy

Crash Tiles is designed for players of all ages and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, please visit our GitHub Issues page.
